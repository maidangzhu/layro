# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Treat other contributors with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, and bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent first
- Focus on the code and the problem
- Give actionable, concrete feedback
- Keep discussions professional and specific

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- abusive language
- personal attacks
- doxxing or publishing private information
- repeated bad-faith disruption

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participation when necessary to protect the project.
